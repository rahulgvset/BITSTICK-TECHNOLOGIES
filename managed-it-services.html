What's included

Managed IT is broader than a one-off fix. Depending on what your business needs, an engagement can include:

  • Helpdesk & end-user support — a place your team can go when something's not working
  • Proactive monitoring — catching problems before they turn into outages
  • Modern workplace management — Microsoft 365, Teams, and SharePoint kept configured, secure, and running smoothly
  • Patch management & updates — software and systems kept current without you having to track it
  • Security monitoring — ongoing attention to the same things we'd check in a one-off cybersecurity audit, but continuously
  • Vendor coordination — one point of contact instead of juggling multiple software and hardware vendors yourself

In-house IT vs. managed services

This is the comparison most businesses are actually making, so here it is directly rather than dodged:

  • In-house hire: full-time salary, benefits, and the risk of a single point of failure if that person is out or leaves
  • Managed services: a team's worth of coverage, without the fixed cost of a full-time role — but less institutional day-to-day presence than someone sitting in your office

For many small businesses, the honest answer is a blend — a lean in-house presence for daily needs, with managed services covering monitoring, security, and specialist depth. That's exactly what the co-managed model above is for.

Managed IT services — FAQs

Q

What is a managed service provider?

A managed service provider (MSP) takes ongoing responsibility for some or all of your IT — monitoring systems, handling support requests, and maintaining infrastructure — instead of you calling someone only when something breaks.

Q

Is managed IT different from IT consulting?

Yes. IT consulting is typically project-based — an audit, a migration, a specific fix. Managed IT is an ongoing relationship where we're actively monitoring and supporting your systems continuously.

Q

Can you work alongside our existing in-house IT team?

Yes — this is co-managed IT. We can take on specific responsibilities like after-hours monitoring or specialist work your team doesn't have bandwidth for, while your team keeps day-to-day ownership.

Q

How does pricing work?

Pricing is scoped to what you actually need after a free consult — we don't publish a flat rate because a fair quote depends on your specific setup.

Q

What happens if something breaks outside business hours?

Coverage depends on the plan you choose — we'll walk through what after-hours support looks like during your consult.
