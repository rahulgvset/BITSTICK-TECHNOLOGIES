IT Consulting for Small and Growing Businesses

An outside technical read on your stack — what's a risk, what's overbuilt, and what to fix first.

Book a free consult See FAQs

An honest outside read, not a sales pitch

Sometimes what you need isn't a build — it's someone with no stake in your existing vendor relationships giving you a straight answer on what's actually working, what's a risk, and what's overbuilt for a business your size.

You get a prioritized, concrete read — not a wall of jargon or an upsell.

How it differs from managed IT services

Consulting is project-based — a focused assessment or a specific decision, not an ongoing relationship. If you want continuous coverage instead, that's what our managed IT services are for.

Assessment only, or implementation too

Some clients want an independent read and nothing more — they'll take our findings to their own team or another vendor. Others want us to carry out the fixes we recommend. Both are common, and we'll scope whichever fits.
