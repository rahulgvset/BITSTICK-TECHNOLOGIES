About us

An IT consulting team that stays after launch.

BITSTICK TECHNOLOGIES works with small and growing businesses that need software and infrastructure they can trust — not just ship and forget. We're an India-based IT consulting team covering application development, cybersecurity, cloud architecture on Azure and AWS, managed IT services, network design, and applied AI.

Our mission

Make good engineering accessible to businesses that don't have an in-house team for it

We were founded on a simple idea: small businesses deserve the same standard of secure, well-built software as large enterprises, without needing to hire a full internal IT department to get it. Every engagement is scoped around what your business actually needs next, not a standard package sold to everyone the same way.

Our team

IT consultants and engineers who stay on the account

Our team is made up of experienced IT consultants and engineers who work directly with clients from the first scoping call through to post-launch support — no account manager relay, no rotating juniors. You talk to the people who actually write the code, configure the infrastructure, and run the security review. That's a deliberate structural choice, not a marketing line: it's why we stay small rather than scale into a layer of account managers between you and the work.

Our services

Application development, cybersecurity, cloud, and applied AI

We offer a full range of IT consulting and development services — application development, network design and implementation, cybersecurity, managed IT services, cloud architecture on Azure and AWS, and AI integration — designed to help small businesses stay competitive and secure. How we're structured

Why we stay small on purpose

Direct access, not a relay chain

Larger IT consulting firms often route client communication through account managers who then check with a technical team. We skip that layer entirely — the engineer scoping your project is the same person building it and answering your questions six months later.

India-based, not India-outsourced

We're not a subcontracted delivery team for someone else's client relationship. Bitstick is the company you're hiring, based in India, working directly with you. See our full Why India page for the economics behind that.

Generalist team, not a single specialist

Because we work across application development, cloud, cybersecurity, and AI rather than one narrow specialty, we can see how a decision in one area affects another — a security choice that changes cloud costs, an architecture decision that affects what AI features are realistic later.

Scoped engagements, not open-ended retainers by default

We'd rather scope a focused project with a clear outcome than sell an open-ended retainer nobody can measure. If ongoing coverage is what you actually need, that becomes managed IT services — a defined, ongoing engagement, not vague hours.
