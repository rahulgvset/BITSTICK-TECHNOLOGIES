About us

An IT team that stays after launch.

BITSTICK TECHNOLOGIES works with small and growing businesses that need software and infrastructure they can trust — not just ship and forget.

Our mission

Make good engineering accessible to businesses that don't have an in-house team for it

We were founded on a simple idea: small businesses deserve the same standard of secure, well-built software as large enterprises, without needing to hire a full internal IT department to get it. Every engagement is scoped around what your business actually needs next, not a standard package.

Our team

Engineers and consultants who stay on the account

Our team is made up of experienced IT professionals who work directly with clients from the first scoping call through to post-launch support — no account manager relay, no rotating juniors. You talk to the people who actually write the code and configure the infrastructure.

Our services

Application development, cybersecurity, cloud, and applied AI

We offer a full range of IT development and consulting services — application development, network design and implementation, cybersecurity, managed IT services, cloud on Azure and AWS, and AI integration — designed to help small businesses stay competitive and secure. See the full list →
